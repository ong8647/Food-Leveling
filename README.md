# Food Leveling 🍽️🐾 — Turn Real Food Into Power!

Food Leveling is a gamified nutrition-inspired adventure where players scan **real-life food** to generate **micropets**—tiny companions that boost the stats of their chosen adventurer. Each food belongs to a nutritional category such as **Carbs, Protein, Fibre, or Fat**, and these determine the traits and buffs of the micropets you obtain.

Build your adventurer, empower them through better food choices, and defeat powerful bosses to earn tokens and unlock new items from the store!

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🧙 Choose Your Main Adventurer
Players start by selecting a unique adventurer, each with different base stats such as:

- **Strength**
- **Speed**
- **Defense**
- **HP**

---

### 🍎 Scan Real-Life Food
Use your device to scan food items. Based on the food category, a corresponding micropet is generated.

- **Carbs →** Energetic micropets that increase **speed**
- **Protein →** Strong and durable micropets that increase **attack**
- **Fibre →** Defensive / cleansing micropets that increase **defense**
- **Fat →** Tanky or high-impact micropets that **might reduce HP**

Each micropet provides buffs to your adventurer and can be upgraded or combined.

---

### 🐾 Micropets as Buffs
Micropets enhance your adventurer with various abilities:

- Attack boosts  
- Healing or shields  
- Speed enhancements  
- Resistances  
- Special effects depending on food quality  

---

### ⚔️ Fight Bosses
Challenge unique bosses inspired by exaggerated food creatures or biological threats.  
Winning boss battles rewards you with **tokens**.

---

### 💰 Earn Tokens & Shop (Future Work)
Tokens earned from fights can be exchanged for:

- Skins / cosmetics  
- Stat boosts  
- Rare micropet eggs  
- Special abilities  
- Limited-time event items  

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Loop

1. **Choose your adventurer**
2. **Scan food to generate micropets**
3. **Enter battles against themed bosses**
4. **Earn tokens upon victory**
5. **Redeem items in the store**
6. **Repeat, grow stronger, unlock more!**
